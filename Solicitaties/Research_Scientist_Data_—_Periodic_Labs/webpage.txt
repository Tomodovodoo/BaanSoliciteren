About Periodic Labs

Periodic Labs is an AI and physical sciences company building state-of-the-art models to accelerate breakthroughs across materials, energy, and related fields.

Role focus

Own evaluations and data across the scientific-AI training stack. Construct evaluations from advanced scientific use cases, source external datasets, integrate internally generated experimental data, build RL training environments, and create a tight feedback loop between scientific use cases, model evaluation, and training data.

Responsibilities

- Own evaluation and data strategy across the training stack.
- Translate scientific workflows into rigorous evaluations, benchmarks, and RL environments.
- Source and assess datasets across chemistry, physics, materials science, mathematics, simulation, and laboratory instrumentation.
- Build pipelines to ingest, clean, transform, and version heterogeneous large-scale training data.
- Build analysis tools for data inspection, model-failure analysis, and evaluation or dataset prioritization.

Desired background

- Evaluation, benchmark, or RL-environment design for language models, agents, or scientific AI.
- Data pipelines for pretraining, midtraining, post-training, or evaluation.
- Judgment about scientific relevance, coverage, provenance, licensing, and contamination.
- Data processing at scale, dataset versioning, and lineage tracking.
- Hypothesis-driven research and controlled experiments.
- Scientific experience in areas including chemistry, materials science, computational physics, semiconductors, or related physical sciences.

Mechanics

- Minimum education: Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
- Locations: Menlo Park, CA or Montreal, Canada; San Francisco planned.
- Compensation: $250,000-$350,000 plus equity.
- Visa sponsorship: available.
